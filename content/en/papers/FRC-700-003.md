# FRC 700.003 v0.1 - Return Is an Update

## 0. Result in one paragraph

In the FRC μ topology, toroidal return means that outward participation can feed a later ground. It does not mean that a system travels around a physical torus or returns unchanged. For a fixed cycle write $z_{n+1}=T(z_n,e_n)$, where $z_n$ is the declared ground-state record and $e_n$ is environmental input. If two reachable intermediate states are collapsed by one selective interface and all later steps are deterministic under the same inputs, the completed cycle also collapses their initial histories. The cycle is therefore noninvertible on those histories. Even without such loss, equality $z_{n+1}=z_n$ is an additional fixed-point or recurrence condition. The default FRC notation is consequently μ0$\to\cdots\to$μ7$\curvearrowright$μ0$'$, with μ0$'$ not presumed equal to μ0.

## 1. Threshold question, scope, and status

**Threshold question.** What makes an outward-and-return process an update rather than a merely circular picture?

The word “toroidal” is restricted here to a conceptual topology: activity passes outward across a declared boundary and can return through a declared channel to condition a later cycle. A return may carry feedback, memory, delay, and environmental disturbance. It may also discard information. None of these properties follows from the shape of an arrow.

This paper provides an exact proposition for composed deterministic maps and a framework record for applications. It does not assert a universal dynamical law, periodicity, conservation, or a physical torus.

## 2. Cycle objects and declared boundary

Let $Z_0$ be the space of ground-state records at the beginning of a cycle. Let $Z_1,\ldots,Z_8$ be intermediate spaces and

$$
\tau_j:Z_j\times E_j\longrightarrow Z_{j+1},
\qquad j=0,\ldots,7, \tag{1}
$$

be threshold maps. The $E_j$ record boundary inputs, controls, or disturbances. For a fixed input sequence $e=(e_0,\ldots,e_7)$, define

$$
T_e=\tau_{7,e_7}\circ\cdots\circ\tau_{0,e_0}:Z_0\to Z_8. \tag{2}
$$

When the next-cycle ground uses the same state type, identify $Z_8$ with $Z_0$ by a declared reset or handoff map. The update is then

$$
z_{n+1}=T(z_n,e_n). \tag{3}
$$

The μ notation labels functional roles in an application; it does not require exactly eight physical transformations. Several μ roles may share a map, one role may require several maps, and an unresolved role may remain absent.

## 3. Return is not inversion

Define the prefix and suffix maps at threshold $j$ by

$$
F_{j,e}=\tau_{j-1,e_{j-1}}\circ\cdots\circ\tau_{0,e_0},
\qquad
G_{j,e}=\tau_{7,e_7}\circ\cdots\circ\tau_{j+1,e_{j+1}}, \tag{4}
$$

with identity maps used at the endpoints. Then

$$
T_e=G_{j,e}\circ\tau_{j,e_j}\circ F_{j,e}. \tag{5}
$$

### Proposition 1. Reachable loss survives the completed return

Suppose there exist $z,z'\in Z_0$ such that

$$
F_{j,e}(z)\ne F_{j,e}(z')
\quad\text{and}\quad
\tau_{j,e_j}(F_{j,e}(z))
=\tau_{j,e_j}(F_{j,e}(z')). \tag{6}
$$

If the downstream map $G_{j,e}$ is a deterministic function under the same fixed inputs, then

$$
T_e(z)=T_e(z'). \tag{7}
$$

Consequently $T_e$ is noninjective on any domain containing $z$ and $z'$, and no inverse on that domain can reconstruct which initial history occurred.

**Proof.** Apply the same function $G_{j,e}$ to both sides of the equality in (6), then use (5). Since the prefix images differ, $z\ne z'$ for deterministic $F_{j,e}$. Thus two distinct initial records have the same completed return. $\square$

The reachability condition is essential. Noninjectivity at states a cycle never visits says nothing about that cycle. The fixed-input condition is also essential: different environmental inputs can carry side information that distinguishes histories.

### Corollary 1. A drawn return arrow does not imply a reversible cycle

If any reachable threshold irreversibly merges histories and no downstream channel reintroduces a distinguishing record, the completed return is not invertible on those histories.

The proposition does not claim every cycle is lossy. It supplies a test: locate the reachable fibers and inspect whether another channel preserves the missing information.

## 4. Four distinct terms

Return descriptions should not collapse the following concepts:

| Term | Role | Minimal declaration |
|---|---|---|
| **Memory** | A state variable carries information from earlier times | Storage variable, update rule, retention time |
| **Feedback** | A measured output influences a later input or control | Sensor/record, comparison or routing rule, actuator/channel |
| **Delay** | Influence arrives after a nonzero interval | Clock, lag distribution or time index |
| **Environment** | Variables outside the declared system affect its transition | Boundary input and coupling rule |

A system may have memory without feedback, feedback without an explicit stored narrative, delay without learning, or environmental input without closed-loop control. FRC's return record must say which one is meant.

## 5. μ0 and the next ground

The conceptual sequence is

$$
\mu0\to\mu1\to\mu2\to\mu3\to\mu4\to\mu5\to\mu6\to\mu7
\curvearrowright\mu0'. \tag{8}
$$

Here μ0$'$ is the prior condition for the next declared cycle. In a physical realization it may include changed state variables, boundary conditions, or spacetime-indexed records. In a formal or organizational application it may be a new initial condition, updated context, or inherited setup. The prime marks temporal and informational succession, not a new universal μ register.

Equality requires a separate condition:

$$
\begin{aligned}
T(z,e)&=z &&\text{for a fixed point},\\
T^p(z)&=z &&\text{for a period-}p\text{ recurrence}.
\end{aligned} \tag{9}
$$

The second line assumes a declared input protocol. Approximate recurrence requires a metric and tolerance. Neither condition follows from (8).

## 6. Return-interface record

| Field | Required declaration |
|---|---|
| Initial ground | State variables represented in μ0 |
| Forward thresholds | Maps and their admissible domains |
| Selection | Retained and discarded information at each interface |
| Outward event | What crosses the μ7 boundary |
| Return carrier | Signal, material, document, rule, or other causal channel |
| Memory | State that persists into the next cycle |
| Delay | Time convention and lag |
| Environment | Inputs held fixed, measured, or unresolved |
| Next ground | How μ0$'$ is constructed |
| Recurrence test | Equality, tolerance, or distributional criterion |
| Failure condition | Observation rejecting the proposed return path |

Without a return carrier, the curved arrow is an interpretive proposal, not a causal statement.

## 7. Three bounded examples

### 7.1 Egg

An egg can be treated as a bounded developing unit whose inward chemical and cellular processes produce outward exchanges with temperature, gases, and handling conditions. Those environmental conditions return as inputs to later development. The next ground is a changed developmental state, not the initial egg. This is an application template; a biological claim requires measured variables and a developmental model.

### 7.2 Research cycle

An idea is developed into a manuscript, exposed to review or readership, and returned as criticism, adoption, citation, or silence. The next working context contains new documents and expectations. A citation does not reconstruct the author's full inward reasoning, and feedback does not guarantee improvement. The causal carriers—messages, edits, repositories, institutions—must be named.

### 7.3 Football match

Players and teams act, scoreboard and crowd events become outward records, and coaching instructions, crowd response, remaining time, and competition context feed later play. A goal at 3–0 changes the score to 3–1, but its functional meaning depends on the remaining trajectory and match constraints. Equal scorelines can arise from different histories; a scoreboard alone does not invert the match.

## 8. Relationship to prior work

FRC 700.001 provides the selective interface whose fibers appear in Proposition 1. FRC 700.002 provides boundary reindexing, so a return may update either the same declared unit or a larger system in which that unit participates. FRC 830's non-reconstruction results independently warn that an outward ledger cannot choose among inward state actions.

Classical cybernetics supplies the broader language of feedback, regulation, and requisite distinction. The present paper's contribution is the scoped FRC notation μ0$'$ and the requirement that a toroidal return specify information loss and causal carriers.

## 9. Valid and prohibited inference

**Valid inference.** If two reachable histories are merged by a declared interface and no separate downstream record distinguishes them, the completed return cannot reconstruct which history occurred.

**Prohibited inference.** Because an account can be drawn as μ0$\to\cdots\to$μ7$\curvearrowright$μ0$'$, the system is periodic, reversible, closed, geometrically toroidal, or governed by one universal dynamics.

## 10. Non-results, failure conditions, and claim status

This paper does not prove that every system cycles, that μ0 is always a physical vacuum state, or that feedback creates meaning, agency, or consciousness. It does not establish a conserved quantity or an exact recurrence.

An application fails if no return carrier can be identified, if its alleged next ground is unaffected by the outward event under controlled comparison, or if environmental information claimed absent actually distinguishes the histories. The framework should be revised if users cannot separate memory, feedback, delay, and environmental input or if μ0$'$ adds no clarity beyond ordinary time indexing.

| Statement | Status |
|---|---|
| Reachable deterministic loss survives composition | Exact formal proposition |
| μ0$'$ denotes the next declared ground | FRC framework definition |
| Equality with μ0 requires a recurrence test | Exact logical distinction |
| Egg, research, and football cycles instantiate the record | Authorial interpretations |
| All return cycles are noninvertible | Not claimed |
| Toroidal means a literal physical torus | Explicitly not claimed |
