## 1. What FRC is: two registers, one architecture

FRC is a status-labeled program structured into **two explicitly distinct registers**:

- **Physics register — Fractal Resonance Coherence (FRC):** Operational coherence observables and open-system entropy bookkeeping; exact and model-specific results in synchronization, chaos, and thermodynamic accounting; residual transport protocols; operational registers (`FRC 700.000–007`); structural obstruction theorems (`FRC 830.000–005`); and the public negative-gate falsification ledger ([https://fractalresonance.com/en/falsifications](https://fractalresonance.com/en/falsifications)).
- **Philosophy register — Fractal Resonance Cognition (FRC):** The philosophical position developed in *The Open System* (*The Unclosed*, `v41`) and *The Mind in the Coupling* (`v3.13`), which analyze persistence as open-system flow and mind as sustained recursive coupling and basin dynamics.

**Boundary ruling:** Coherence lives in physics; cognition lives in philosophy. The physics equation $dS + k^*\,d\ln C = 0$ does **not** claim to prove consciousness, subjective experience, or AI personhood. Philosophical coherence across the monographs never substitutes for empirical or mathematical proof in physics.

FRC does **not** present its components as one completed physical theory. Every claim must be read under its declared epistemic label:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Definition | A convention, notation, register assignment, or analysis rule adopted by FRC. |
| Mathematical result | A theorem, exact identity, or structural obstruction proved within stated hypotheses. |

| Conjecture | A proposed physical extension not established by the available results. |
| Philosophy register | The interpretive position of Fractal Resonance Cognition (*The Open System*, *The Mind in the Coupling*); not physical evidence. |

A successful model, exact identity, or philosophical analogy never silently promotes a conjecture into an established law of nature.

## 2. Canonical relation

FRC's canonical relation in the physics register is

$$
dS + k^*\,d\ln C = 0. \tag{1}
$$

Its **form is scale invariant**. The starred quantity $k^*$ is the Boltzmann-shaped bridge that makes a declared entropy channel and logarithmic coherence channel commensurable. Do **not** interpret $k^*$ as:

- one universal fixed numerical scalar across all units;
- an outcome-fitted parameter;
- a value recomputed at each time step or state;
- the Standard Map kick strength $K_{\mathrm{drive}}$;
- a generic simulation normalization or kernel weight.

In an operational realization, the representation is written $k^*_\mu$ (or $k^*_r$), where the index records the declared realization: units, scale, observables, boundary, and sign convention (for example, $k^*_{\mu_{\mathrm{nat}}} = 1$ in a declared information-nat realization, or $k^*_{\mu_{\mathrm{th}}} = k_B$ in a thermodynamic-unit realization). The subscript records the realization; it neither alters the canonical form nor permits fitting the bridge to an outcome.

Eq. (1) carries two distinct statuses that must never be collapsed:

1. **Operational:** At a declared boundary, Eq. (1) is used as a bookkeeping identity and control-law form.
2. **Conjectural:** That the same invariant form governs real open physical systems generally remains an open physical conjecture.

## 3. Operational requirements and boundary accounting

Before any FRC relation is evaluated, seven items must be fixed or declared prior to scoring:

1. System and boundary.
2. Entropy channel $S$, including units and estimator.
3. Positive coherence channel $C$, including normalization and observable definition.
4. Operational representation of $k^*$.
5. Environment or exchange channels represented by the model.
6. Sign convention and any residual definition.
7. Controls, equal-information baselines, and failure criteria.

Entropy and coherence channels must be operationally independent. Defining $C = \exp(-S/k^*)$ and treating Eq. (1) as an empirical test is circular.

For a modeled subsystem, standard nonequilibrium accounting remains

$$
dS_{\mathrm{sys}} = d_i S + d_e S, \qquad d_i S \ge 0. \tag{2}
$$

The system-only quantity

$$
Q_{\mathrm{sys}} = S_{\mathrm{sys}} + k^*\ln C \tag{3}
$$

is a diagnostic. Motion of $Q_{\mathrm{sys}}$ indicates that the chosen system-only ledger is unclosed; it identifies missing exchange or boundary accounting and does not by itself prove or refute the universal open-system conjecture. An identification such as $\lambda = -d_e S$ is admissible only when it follows from an explicitly declared boundary convention.

## 4. Coherence, entropy, and Lambda notation

$C$ is never automatically one universal substance; it is an operationally declared coherence quantity. In phase systems it may be the Kuramoto order-parameter magnitude:

$$
C = \left|\frac{1}{N}\sum_{j=1}^{N} e^{i\phi_j}\right|, \qquad C \in (0, 1]. \tag{4}

Other domains require their own predeclared observable channel and normalization.

FRC separates three Lambda objects:

Success of $\Lambda_{\mathrm{obs}}$, $\Lambda_{\mathrm{eq}}$, or a useful $\Lambda_{\mathrm{dyn}}$ closure does **not** establish a fundamental spacetime Lambda field, which remains a separate conjecture.
