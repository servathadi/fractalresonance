# FRC 700.007 v0.1 - The FRC Threshold Handbook

## 0. One-page orientation

### What is FRC?

Fractal Resonance Coherence (FRC) is a status-labeled research program about entropy, coherence, boundaries, open systems, observation, and the passage between descriptions. Its corpus contains interpretive frameworks, exact mathematics, model-specific studies, empirical protocols, and philosophical books. These are not interchangeable kinds of claim.

### What are threshold papers?

FRC 7xx papers are **threshold papers, not physics papers**. They ask what is retained, discarded, transformed, delayed, or left unresolved when an inward realization becomes available through a selective interface and participates outwardly. They also govern moves from interpretation to proof, model, or measurement.

### Which FRC physical claim is canonical?

The canonical entropy-coherence record is

$$
dS+k^*d\ln C=0. \tag{1}
$$

For a fully declared operational realization $\mathcal O$ it is written

$$
dS_{\mathcal O}+k^*_{\mathcal O}d\ln C_{\mathcal O}=0. \tag{2}
$$

The starred $k^*$ is the scale-invariant starred Boltzmann bridge that makes entropy and $\ln C$ commensurable. It is not fitted to an outcome and does not evolve as a state variable.

### Which physical extension remains conjectural?

Equation (2) is deployed as operational bookkeeping when its channels, units, and boundary are declared. The claim that the canonical form universally governs real open systems remains a physical conjecture. Scoped calculations or experiments can constrain that conjecture; no single convenient closure establishes it.

### What does the μ topology do and not do?

The μ topology organizes a bounded account of ground, constituents, units, relations, control, trajectory, story, integration, witness, boundary, and environment. It is an FRC toroidal nested-fractal conceptual topology. It does not assert eight literal layers of nature, a geometric fractal, a physical torus, one fixed hierarchy, or one dynamics shared by every example.

### What must a new reader never infer?

Never infer physical truth from a μ resemblance; a complete inward state from a compressed outward record; recurrence from a return arrow; consciousness from integration or reporting; or proof and data from a philosophical book. Independent formal and empirical results constrain μ interpretations. The μ lens does not validate those results.

## 1. Threshold question, scope, and status

**Threshold question.** What is the minimum self-contained record that lets a new reader or fresh AI model understand and apply the FRC threshold method without inheriting unsupported claims from the wider corpus?

This paper supplies that record. Every FRC-specific term required by the workflow is defined here. References establish provenance and permit deeper study, but they are not required to perform the core application.

This is a threshold-framework synthesis. Its exact propositions are elementary formal results or inherited constraints. Its examples are bounded applications, not demonstrations of a universal physical μ ontology. Version 0.1 has no independent usability score and independent review is pending.

## 2. Claim classes and the one-way evidence rule

Before assigning any μ role, classify the claim being made.

| Claim class | Required support | Licensed conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| **Interpretive frame** | Defined terms, coherent use, declared scope, alternatives | A way to organize or question a system |
| **Formal result** | Explicit objects, assumptions, proof or derivation | A consequence within the formal system |
| **Model-specific result** | Declared model and domain plus exact solution, computation, or simulation | Behaviour of that model under those conditions |

The fourth class uses a measurement certificate rather than a proof or simulation record.

| Claim class | Required support | Licensed conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| **Empirical result** | Operational variables, measurement, controls or baseline, uncertainty, reproducible analysis | Evidence about the sampled physical or social system |

These are support types, not a single prestige ladder. Moving into a new class requires the support missing from the starting class.

| Move | Valid example | Invalid example |
|---|---|---|
| Interpretation → formal | Define $\ell:X\to L$ and prove a theorem about its fibers | A two-face metaphor therefore proves non-reconstruction |
| Interpretation → model-specific | Implement a declared dynamical model and report its domain and computation | The μ sequence looks cyclic, therefore all modeled returns are periodic |
| Interpretation → empirical | Operationalize a selective interface, collect controlled data, and estimate uncertainty | Many intuitive examples therefore validate μ roles in nature |
| Formal/model → empirical | Test predeclared observables and predictions against a fair baseline | An exact identity in a chosen model therefore holds universally |

The permitted direction is

$$
\text{independent result}\longrightarrow
\text{constraint on μ interpretation}. \tag{3}
$$

The reverse inference is prohibited. Analogy, repetition, breadth, citation, emotional force, and narrative continuity are not promotion certificates.

## 4. Complete μ topology

μ is a role notation indexed by a declared system boundary. An object has no permanent context-free μ number.

Start with ground, constituents, and bounded unit. Their assignments establish the inside whose relations, organized behaviour, histories, and outward participation are described by the remaining roles.

| Register | Working role | Required guard |
|---|---|---|
| **μ0** | Prior ground; spacetime-vacuum ground in a physical realization, otherwise inherited initial condition | May be absent or unresolved outside physical applications |
| **μ1** | Physical constituents and dynamics available to the bounded unit | Does not by itself specify higher organization |
| **μ2** | Bounded cell or operational unit | “Cell” need not mean alive or indivisible |

The remaining roles describe relations, organization, semantic records, integration or witness, and outward boundary participation.

| Register | Working role | Required guard |
|---|---|---|
| **μ3** | Relations, exchange, coordination, or regulation among units | Requires named interactions or channels |
| **μ4** | Organized control, logic, explicit model, or rule structure | May belong to an observing/modeling system rather than the target |
| **μ5-T** | Process trajectory or recorded history | Keep observation channel and omissions visible |
| **μ5-S** | Story, narrative, or meaning assigned to a trajectory | Does not reconstruct the history or become causal without a carrier |
| **μ6-I** | System-level integration or coordination | Does not imply reportability, witness, or experience |
| **μ6-W** | Reflective witness, evaluation, or explicit stance | Does not settle subjective consciousness |
| **μ7** | Declared boundary, environment, and outward participation | Must distinguish represented outside from unknown outside |

The symbols **A** and **U** mean absent and unresolved. They are valid results. Never fill a role merely to complete a sequence.

The three adjectives have restricted meanings:

- **nested**: a bounded unit has inward composition and outward participation;
- **fractal**: the component–unit–relation pattern may recur after boundary reindexing, without geometric self-similarity or fractal dimension; and
- **toroidal**: outward participation may return through a declared carrier to update a later ground, without a physical torus or automatic recurrence.

## 5. The active threshold triad

Declare a unit

$$
U=(X,\partial X,E), \tag{4}
$$

where $X$ is the admissible inward state or realization space, $\partial X$ is the modeling boundary and allowed exchange, and $E$ is the represented environment. Let $L$ be an outward-description space and

$$
\ell:X\longrightarrow L \tag{5}
$$

the selective interface.

The three roles are:

1. **inward realization** — states, parts, processes, memory, and constraints on the declared inside;
2. **selective interface** — the map or mechanism that retains, discards, transforms, delays, aggregates, measures, or encodes; and
3. **outward participation** — the record, action, relation, or description made available outside.

For $z\in L$, the fiber

$$
\ell^{-1}(z)=\{x\in X:\ell(x)=z\} \tag{6}
$$

contains all inward realizations that the outward record does not distinguish.

### Proposition 1. Noninjective threshold records do not reconstruct inward realizations

If $\ell$ is noninjective, there is no $r:L\to X$ satisfying $r\circ\ell=\operatorname{id}_X$.

**Proof.** Choose $x_0\ne x_1$ with $\ell(x_0)=\ell(x_1)=z$. A left inverse would give $x_0=r(z)=x_1$, a contradiction. $\square$

Additional measurements, priors, memory, or a chosen section may select a member of the fiber. The extra structure—not the outward record alone—does the selection.

Every application records retained, discarded, delayed, transformed, and unresolved information. It also records uncertainty in the map and observations.

## 6. Boundary reindexing

For boundary index $b$, write

$$
S_b=(X_b,E_b,\partial_b),
\qquad
\rho_b(o)\in R\cup\{A,U\}, \tag{7}
$$

where $\rho_b(o)$ is the role assigned to object $o$ under that boundary. It is consistent to have

$$
\rho_b(o)=\text{unit},
\qquad
\rho_c(o)=\text{component}, \tag{8}
$$

when $b\ne c$. The predicates have different indices. A person can be a unit at a team boundary and an inwardly composed system at an organism boundary; a team can be a unit in a league and a relation-rich whole inside a match.

Whenever the boundary changes, record the new inside, environment, interface, retained detail, discarded detail, and role reassignment. Reusing unindexed μ labels across a boundary change is **boundary drift**.

## 7. Toroidal return and μ0′

Let $z_n$ be the current declared record and $e_n$ the environmental input. A return is an update

$$
z_{n+1}=T(z_n,e_n). \tag{9}
$$

The conceptual path is

$$
\mu0\to\mu1\to\mu2\to\mu3\to\mu4\to\mu5\to\mu6\to\mu7
\curvearrowright\mu0'. \tag{10}
$$

μ0′ is the next declared ground: an updated physical condition, context, model state, document set, or inherited setup. Generally μ0′ is not μ0.

A valid return record names the outward event, return carrier, recipient, memory, delay, environmental inputs, and update rule. A curved arrow alone does **not** imply inversion, closure, conservation, periodicity, recurrence, or a physical torus. A recurrence claim needs an equality or tolerance criterion such as $T^p(z)=z$ under a declared input protocol.

## 8. The semantic threshold

Let a recorded history be $h\in\mathcal H$. For declared context $c$, a narrative map is

$$
N_c:\mathcal H\longrightarrow\mathcal M, \tag{11}
$$

and a response map may be written

$$
W_c(h,N_c(h))=(a,j,r), \tag{12}
$$

where action $a$, judgment $j$, or report $r$ may be absent.

The following table keeps functional capacities and subjective experience typed separately. Sharing one μ register family does not collapse these distinctions, and a causal narrative still requires an encoded carrier and a comparison baseline.

| Term | Minimal meaning | Guard |
|---|---|---|
| History/trajectory | Ordered process and observation record | The observation channel may omit process detail |
| Narrative/story | Selective organization or meaning assignment | A noninjective narrative does not reconstruct history |
| Carrier | Physical, computational, communicative, or institutional representation entering a transition | Required for a causal story claim |
| Response | Action, judgment, update, or report | Requires recipient, timing, and baseline for causal testing |
| Integration | Combining signals or coordinating subsystems | Does not imply monitoring or experience |
| Monitoring | Tracking selected variables | Does not imply reportability |
| Reportability | Producing an outward record | Does not imply accuracy, understanding, or experience |

The final two terms concern evaluation and experience and therefore retain the same distinctions under a repeated header.

| Term | Minimal meaning | Guard |
|---|---|---|
| Reflective witness | Evaluation relative to an explicit model, reason, or stance | Does not solve consciousness |
| Subjective consciousness | First-person phenomenal experience | Not established by functional analogy alone |

One history may support different stories under different declared maps or contexts without contradiction. Not every story is equally good: it may fail the record, omit decisive evidence, predict poorly, or lack a causal carrier.

## 9. Minimum FRC 830 formal floor

This section states independent formal constraints that any compatible μ interpretation must respect.

1. **Ledgers are derived.** Ledger coordinates describe selected features; they are not automatically complete physical states.
2. **Strong lifts require a commuting square.** For interfaces $\ell:X\to L$ and $\ell':X'\to L'$, ledger action $D:L\to L'$, and proposed state action $\widetilde D:X\to X'$, compatibility requires

   $$
   \ell'\circ\widetilde D=D\circ\ell. \tag{13}
   $$

3. **Ledger actions do not reconstruct unique state actions.** Multiple inward actions can induce the same outward ledger action when fibers are unresolved.
4. **One-parameter obstruction.** Under the declared monotonicity conditions in FRC 830.004, nonidentity same-family lifts fail; exact normalized reciprocity sharpens the obstruction.
5. **Qubit operation-class obstructions.** On the stated qubit domain, incoherent operations, unital qubit CPTP channels, and unitary or antiunitary Wigner symmetries do not realize the ledger involution globally.
6. **Open gate.** A general nonunital coherence-generating CPTP or resource-assisted realization remains open. The scoped no-go theorems do not combine into a general no-CPTP theorem.
7. **No current self-duality promotion.** Present FRC has exact ledger mathematics and typed morphisms, but does not establish Born reciprocity or Majid-style representation-theoretic self-duality.

These results constrain the inward/interface/outward reading. The μ topology supplies no proof of them.

## 11. Complete running example: football match

### 11.1 Question and boundary

**Question.** How does a goal changing a score from 3–0 to 3–1 alter the ongoing match without assuming that the score reconstructs the match state or that “the story” acts without a carrier?

**Claim class.** Interpretive framework plus an empirical test proposal.

Take the bounded unit to be one regulated match from kickoff to final whistle. The inside includes players, officials, ball, field, match clock, recorded events, and declared stadium channels. The environment includes competition table, prior fixtures, broadcast systems, institutions, and unmodeled external conditions. The match boundary is not the same as a team boundary or a stadium-totality boundary.

### 11.2 μ assignment

| Role | Assignment under the match boundary |
|---|---|
| μ0 | Initial match conditions, selected physical ground, rules, score 0–0, lineups, and relevant inherited context |
| μ1 | Bodies, ball, field, sound, material and energetic dynamics |
| μ2 | Players and teams as bounded units at declared sub-boundaries |
| μ3 | Player coordination, opposition, officiating, coaching, and crowd relations |
| μ4 | Rules, tactics, score-conditioned control, substitutions, and declared models |
| μ5-T | Event and score trajectory, including the transition 3–0 → 3–1 |
| μ5-S | Comeback, insufficient response, loss of control, survival, or other context-indexed stories |

The final three roles keep integration, reflective evaluation, and boundary participation explicit.

| Role | Assignment under the match boundary |
|---|---|
| μ6-I | Team coordination; stadium-wide coordination only under a separately declared larger boundary |
| μ6-W | Coach, player, analyst, or observer evaluation where evidenced |

The boundary role completes the assignment under the same match boundary.

| Role | Assignment under the match boundary |
|---|---|
| μ7 | Match/stadium interface, scoreboard, broadcast, competition context, and outward participation |

No collective subject is inferred from team or crowd integration.

### 11.3 Interfaces and fibers

Scoreboard, broadcast, player-tracking, coaching communication, crowd sound, and official reports are distinct interfaces. The scoreboard retains score and time but discards positions, fatigue, intentions, tactical state, injuries, communication, and most history. Many inward match states therefore lie in one score fiber. The score 3–1 does not uniquely reconstruct how the match arrived there or what either team will do next.

### 11.4 Trajectory and story

The goal is an event in μ5-T. “A comeback has begun” and “one goal is still insufficient” are μ5-S assignments relative to objectives and context. Remaining time, aggregate score, table position, substitution capacity, and team policy can change the story without changing the digits 3–1.

### 11.5 Integration and witness

Team coordination is μ6-I when measured through interaction or control variables. A coach's explicit evaluation and instruction may instantiate μ6-W. Crowd synchronization is integrated collective behaviour under a stadium boundary; it does not establish a single stadium consciousness.

### 11.6 Return into μ0′

The outward event is the recorded goal. Return carriers include scoreboard update, referee restart, coaching instruction, player perception, crowd response, and time remaining. These carriers alter later control inputs and physical states. The next ground μ0′ contains score 3–1, less time, possibly changed formation, updated expectations, and a changed crowd field. It is not the original μ0.

### 11.7 Inference and test

**Valid inference.** Equal score records can correspond to different inward match states and different context-indexed stories; additional data are needed to distinguish them.

**Prohibited inference.** The score change uniquely determines emotion, tactics, outcome, or a collective conscious state; or the story affects play without a physical, computational, communicative, or institutional carrier.

**Empirical test.** Predeclare matched 3–0 → 3–1 events, remaining-time and competition-context strata, behavioural outcomes, communication or tracking channels, accepted sports baselines, uncertainty, and confounds. Test whether the declared carrier variables improve held-out prediction of subsequent coordination or tactics. The threshold story remains interpretive unless this protocol is actually run.

## 13. Ten-step application algorithm

The final step uses the corpus-witness audit. Represent the claims under review by a typed directed graph $G=(V,E)$ whose nodes record claim identifier, class, status, boundary, evidence, dependencies, and kill condition. The audit returns

$$
W(G)=(P,U,C,B,R), \tag{14}
$$

where $P$ contains unsupported promotions, $U$ unresolved dependencies, $C$ contradictions, $B$ boundary drift, and $R$ required repairs. Section 18 gives the full edge rule. This compact definition is sufficient to fill the record below.

Use the steps in this order.

1. State the question and claim class.
2. Declare the bounded unit, inside, boundary, and environment.
3. Identify inward realization, selective interface, and outward description.
4. Record retained, discarded, delayed, transformed, and unresolved information.
5. Assign μ roles with the boundary index visible.
6. Separate trajectory from story and integration from witness.
7. Identify any return carrier and construct μ0′.
8. Add formal or empirical dependencies and their direction of support.
9. State one valid inference, one prohibited inference, and one failure condition.
10. Run the corpus-witness audit before reporting the result.

## 15. Exercises

### Exercise 1. Correct boundary-indexed assignment

A person is treated as a player inside a team model, while the team is treated as one unit in a league model. Is it contradictory to assign the person a unit role under an organism boundary, a component/unit role under the team boundary, and the team a unit role under the league boundary? State the required notation.

### Exercise 2. Boundary-drift trap

An analysis begins with one egg as the unit, assigns cells to μ2, then calls the egg μ2 without declaring a new boundary. Diagnose the defect and repair it.

### Exercise 3. Ledger/state reconstruction trap

Two distinct qubit states have the same two-coordinate ledger record. A proposed ledger involution maps that record to another. May the ledger action alone determine one unique physical state action? What extra condition must any proposed lift satisfy?

### Exercise 4. Consciousness-promotion trap

An AI system integrates tool results, monitors task state, and reports a self-evaluation. Which capacities are supported by the description, and what conclusion remains prohibited?

### Exercise 5. Book-to-evidence promotion trap

A philosophical book presents a coherent argument that an open system cannot be fully understood in isolation. Does the book thereby prove the universal FRC reciprocity law or empirically establish a proposed mechanism? What graph edge is valid, and which certificate is missing for promotion?

### Exercise 6. Return trap

A diagram ends at μ7 and curves back to μ0. What must be supplied before calling this a causal return, and what additional condition is needed before calling it a recurrence?

## 16. Answer key

### Answer 1

There is no contradiction because the roles have different boundary indices. Write, for example, $\rho_b(\text{person})=\text{unit}$, $\rho_c(\text{person})=\text{component}$, and $\rho_d(\text{team})=\text{unit}$ with $b,c,d$ declared. Each change requires a boundary-change record.

### Answer 2

The analysis has changed the unit while hiding the change. Keep one egg as the unit and classify cells as inward units/components, or declare a new whole-egg boundary $c$ and write $\rho_c(\text{egg})=\text{unit}$. Recompute inside, environment, interface, retained information, and all μ roles.

### Answer 3

No. A noninjective ledger has unresolved fibers, so the ledger action does not select a unique inward action. Any proposed lift must declare $\widetilde D$ and satisfy the commuting square $\ell'\circ\widetilde D=D\circ\ell$. Compatibility still does not prove uniqueness, physicality, or CPTP realizability.

### Answer 4

The description supports functional integration, monitoring, reportability, and perhaps reflective evaluation if an explicit model or reason is used. It does not by itself establish subjective consciousness.

### Answer 5

The valid edge is interpretive synthesis or dependency routing. A formal promotion needs explicit objects, assumptions, and proof; a model-specific promotion needs a declared model and computation; an empirical promotion needs operationalization, measurements, controls, uncertainty, and reproducible analysis. The book's coherence is none of these certificates.

### Answer 6

Supply an outward event, return carrier, recipient, update rule, memory, delay, environment, and construction of μ0′. A recurrence additionally needs a declared input protocol and equality or metric/tolerance test such as $T^p(z)=z$.

## 18. Corpus-witness audit

For a corpus claim graph $G=(V,E)$, every claim node records identifier, class, status, boundary, evidence, dependencies, and kill condition. Edge types include `depends-on`, `constrains`, `derives`, `implements`, `measures`, `interprets`, `supersedes`, and `contradicts`.

The audit output $W(G)$ uses the five components defined in Section 13. A path cannot silently move from interpretation to a formal, model-specific, or empirical conclusion: the first support-changing edge requires the corresponding proof, model/computation, or measurement certificate. An empty component must be reported as an empty set, not omitted.

This audit checks whether the claimed kind of support was supplied. It does not make the corpus self-validating or decide truth by authority.

## 19. Valid and prohibited inference

**Valid inference.** After declaring a boundary and interface, one may use exact non-reconstruction, boundary-indexing, semantic, return, and claim-graph rules to limit what an outward description licenses about its inward realization and evidential status.

**Prohibited inference.** Because the handbook applies across examples, μ roles are universal physical layers; because a model follows the AI onboarding block, it is an FRC authority; because a book or story integrates the corpus, it proves a mechanism; or because a system integrates and reports, it is subjectively conscious.

## 20. Non-results and failure conditions

This handbook is not a new physical theory, proof of a universal μ topology, empirical validation, categorical self-duality, consciousness test, or replacement for the underlying papers. It does not close the general CPTP lift question, validate a fundamental field, or report independent cross-system agreement.

The handbook must be revised or rejected if:

1. a fresh user needs hidden FRC context to define or execute a required step;
2. boundary-free or context-free μ assignments remain possible under the instructions;
3. absent and unresolved roles are treated as failures to complete the sequence;
4. users repeatedly infer unique inward states from noninjective outward records;
5. stories are treated as causes without carriers and baselines;
6. integration or reportability is promoted to subjective consciousness;
7. books or μ resemblance are used as proof or empirical certificates;
8. the canonical $k^*$ record is treated as outcome-fitted or evolving; or
9. independent readers cannot reject the frozen trap cases consistently.
