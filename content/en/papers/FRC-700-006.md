# FRC 700.006 v0.1 - The Reflexive Threshold

## 0. Result in one paragraph

When FRC itself is the declared unit, its μ6 role can be made precise as an auditable operation: integrate claims while preserving their classes, dependencies, boundaries, contradictions, and kill conditions. This paper represents the corpus by a typed claim graph $G$, attaches certificates to class-promoting edges, and defines a witness audit $W(G)$. The no-silent-promotion theorem then states that a path made only of citation, interpretation, dependency, or synthesis cannot by itself license a formal, model-specific, or empirical conclusion. This is an exact result about the declared governance system. It does not prove any FRC physical conjecture, make the corpus self-validating, or attribute subjective consciousness to a corpus, author, or AI.

## 1. Threshold question, scope, and status

**Threshold question.** When FRC itself is treated as a bounded unit, what operation legitimately occupies the μ6 role, and how can it integrate the corpus without promoting interpretation into proof or evidence?

FRC 7xx papers are **threshold papers, not physics papers**. Their task is to record what happens when an argument crosses among an inward realization, a selective interface, and an outward description or participation. Here the active threshold is reflexive: papers, books, experiments, and reviews become objects inside a declared corpus boundary, while readers, reviewers, measurements, and publication systems participate from outside it.

The answer is procedural. The μ6 role is assigned to a status-preserving audit process, not to one person, one model, or a hidden center. The process integrates, compares, challenges, and repairs the corpus. It may expose whether the corpus supplied the support it claims to have supplied. It cannot decide truth by authority.

The μ topology remains an FRC toroidal nested-fractal conceptual topology. It is not a claim that nature is divided into literal levels. This paper is a working draft, with independent proof review and independent methodological review pending.

## 2. Declared unit and boundary

Let $F_b$ denote the FRC corpus under a declared boundary $b$. The boundary includes the source set, versions, registries, and audit date. A later release or an external result may therefore define a different unit $F_{b'}$.

| Register | Authorial assignment for $F_b$ |
|---|---|
| μ0 | Prior repository, scientific, philosophical, and historical conditions from which a corpus revision begins |
| μ1 | Physical and informational carriers: people, computers, repositories, documents, measurements, and experimental materials |
| μ2 | Bounded corpus units: papers, books, experiments, datasets, websites, releases, and task-agents |
| μ3 | Citations, dependencies, revisions, reviews, implementations, measurements, and cross-document relations |
| μ4 | Canonical equations, definitions, proofs, models, status rules, registries, and verification logic |
| μ5 | Research trajectory and narratives used to interpret that trajectory, kept distinct |
| μ6 | Integration, status-preserving review, contradiction detection, reflective audit, and refusal of invalid inference |
| μ7 | Readers, reviewers, scientific community, publishing and indexing systems, external evidence, and the declared corpus boundary |
| μ0′ | Corrected corpus state after feedback returns through a governed revision |

This assignment is authorial and auditable, not ontologically privileged. Changing the boundary requires reindexing the roles. A paper may be a μ2 unit inside $F_b$ and the enclosing unit of its own internal argument under another boundary.

## 3. Typed claim graph

### 3.1 Graph and node record

Represent a finite audited snapshot by a directed multigraph

$$
G=(V,E), \tag{1}
$$

where parallel edges are allowed because one claim may both depend on and interpret another. Every node $v\in V$ carries

$$
q(v)=(i,t,s,b,e,d,k). \tag{2}
$$

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| $i$ | Stable document and claim identifier |
| $t$ | Claim class: interpretive, formal, model-specific, or empirical |
| $s$ | Status such as definition, proved candidate, simulated, measured, conjectural, philosophical, contradicted, superseded, or unresolved |
| $b$ | Declared system, operational realization, and boundary |
| $e$ | Evidence, derivation, computation, or measurement record actually supplied |
| $d$ | Dependencies and their versions |
| $k$ | Failure, falsification, or kill condition |

The graph does not collapse all support into one scalar rank. Formal proof, model calculation, and empirical measurement answer different questions. “Promotion” below means a change from an interpretive statement into a class that claims one of those certified supports, or a change among certified classes that claims support of a new kind.

### 3.2 Edge types

Every edge $a\in E$ has one or more declared types from

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{type}(a)\in\{&\text{depends-on},\text{constrains},
\text{derives},\text{implements},\\
&\text{measures},\text{interprets},\text{supersedes},
\text{contradicts}\}.
\end{aligned} \tag{3}
$$

The first question is not whether an edge sounds reasonable. It is what operation the edge claims to perform. A citation can establish provenance; it cannot substitute for the cited proof or measurement. An interpretation may organize results; it cannot silently change their evidence class.

### 3.3 Promotion certificates

Only three support-bearing target classes require evidential promotion certificates.

A certificate belongs to the edge where the support type changes. It must identify the exact proof, model record, or measurement record being invoked, so that a later auditor can inspect the transition without reconstructing the author's intention.

| Target class | Required promotion certificate |
|---|---|
| Formal | Explicit objects, assumptions, and proof or derivation |
| Model-specific | Declared model and domain plus an exact solution, computation, or simulation record |
| Empirical | Operationalization, measurement, controls or baseline, uncertainty, and reproducible analysis |

Citations, synthesis, analogy, narrative continuity, μ assignment, authorial confidence, and book authority are **not** promotion certificates. A valid formal certificate does not automatically become an empirical certificate, and empirical fit does not automatically prove a formal mechanism.

## 4. Corpus-witness audit

Define

$$
W(G)=(P,U,C,B,R), \tag{4}
$$

where:

- $P$ is the set of unsupported promotion edges;
- $U$ is the set of unresolved, missing, or version-unstable dependencies;
- $C$ is the set of explicit logical or status-level contradictions;
- $B$ is the set of boundary, unit, or operational-register drifts; and
- $R$ is the set of required repairs, questions, tests, or review gates.

The audit output is itself a record, not a verdict of truth. A claim may be well certified and false because its assumptions or measurements fail. A claim may also be true but uncertified in the current corpus. $W$ distinguishes those states instead of rewarding rhetorical confidence.

### 4.1 Audit rules

For each node and edge, the auditor must:

1. resolve the cited version and boundary;
2. compare the claimed edge operation with the supplied record;
3. demand the class-specific certificate whenever the target claims new support;
4. preserve explicit contradictions and unresolved items rather than smoothing them into a story;
5. record the repair needed to reopen a rejected transition; and
6. disclose whether the auditor is author, collaborator, tool, independent reviewer, or automated check.

Automated checks may locate missing records and inconsistent strings. Authoring AIs may perform useful adversarial review. Neither is independent proof review merely because it is critical or reproducible.

## 5. No-silent-promotion theorem

### Theorem 1. No silent promotion

Let

$$
\pi:v_0\xrightarrow{a_1}v_1\xrightarrow{a_2}\cdots
\xrightarrow{a_n}v_n \tag{5}
$$

be a path in a claim graph governed by Sections 3 and 4. If $v_n$ claims a formal, model-specific, or empirical support class not warranted at $v_0$ by that path's initial record, then $\pi$ licenses the change only if it contains an edge carrying the certificate required for the target support. If no such certificate occurs, at least one edge of $\pi$ lies in $P$.

**Proof.** Suppose the path claims a new support class at its end. Inspect the path from $v_0$ and choose the first edge $a_j$ whose target $v_j$ claims support of a kind not warranted by $v_{j-1}$ and the preceding edges. By the graph rule, that change requires the corresponding proof, model/computation, or measurement certificate. If $a_j$ carries it, the class change is explicit and may be audited on its merits. If $a_j$ does not carry it, the definition of $P$ places $a_j$ among the unsupported promotion edges. Therefore a certificate-free path cannot license the promoted endpoint. $\square$

This is a formal governance theorem about a defined graph discipline. It is not a theorem about nature, scientific truth, or the sociology of review. Its purpose is to make the evidence-direction rule of FRC 700.000 executable across long dependency paths.

### 5.1 Minimal path check

A binary implementation can mark the first interpretive-to-certified transition lacking a certificate. A numerical encoding may be used for a software spot check, but its integers do not rank proof, models, and measurements by truth or importance. The manuscript's rule is certificate-specific.

## 7. Four live transition audits

### 7.1 FRC 566: bookkeeping to physical conjecture

**Source node.** For a declared operational realization $\mathcal O$, FRC records

$$
dS_{\mathcal O}+k^*_{\mathcal O}d\ln C_{\mathcal O}=0, \tag{6}
$$

where $k^*_{\mathcal O}$ is the scale-invariant starred Boltzmann bridge. It is never fitted to an outcome or allowed to evolve as a state variable.

**Valid edge.** `derives`: declared $S_{\mathcal O}$ and $C_{\mathcal O}$ plus an exact derivation may establish the relation as operational bookkeeping on that realization.

**Invalid edge.** `interprets`: operational bookkeeping $\to$ universal law governing real open systems.

**Audit.** The latter requires model-specific and empirical certificates over declared domains. Until supplied, $P$ contains that promotion and $R$ preserves the open-system statement as a conjecture. No contradiction is implied by leaving it open.

### 7.2 FRC 830: ledger involution to physical self-duality

**Source node.** The normalized ledger has the exact coordinate involution

$$
D(s,y)=(-y,-s). \tag{7}
$$

**Valid edge.** `constrains`: the state/ledger commuting-square and non-reconstruction results restrict any μ interpretation that treats the outward ledger as a complete inward state.

**Invalid edge.** `interprets`: an involution on two ledger coordinates $\to$ a physical state-space action, Born reciprocity, or Majid-style self-duality.

**Audit.** $P$ records the missing state-space or categorical certificate. $U$ retains the general nonunital coherence-generating CPTP and resource-assisted realization questions as open. FRC 830.004 separately excludes incoherent, unital qubit CPTP, and unitary or antiunitary Wigner-class realizations on its stated domains. Those no-go results cannot be combined into a general no-CPTP theorem.

### 7.3 FRC 787: observation target to fundamental field

**Source node.** The paper separates an observation-derived quantity $\Lambda_{\rm obs}$, an equilibrium target $\Lambda_{\rm eq}$, and optional latent dynamics $\Lambda_{\rm dyn}$.

**Valid edge.** `implements`: a predeclared latent equation may be analyzed as a model-specific dynamics and compared with its target and observations.

**Invalid edge.** `interprets`: useful latent dynamics $\to$ a fundamental physical field.

**Audit.** $B$ catches any change from a boundary-relative diagnostic to an undeclared universal field. $P$ records the absent empirical and field-theoretic certificate. $R$ demands a separate physical model, observables, dimensional analysis, domain, and discriminating tests.

### 7.4 Books: synthesis to mechanism or evidence

**Source node.** A book integrates philosophical arguments, scientific sources, and status-labeled FRC claims into a larger account.

**Valid edge.** `interprets`: the book may expose tensions, propose questions, compare perspectives, and route the reader to the underlying paper or established science.

**Invalid edge.** `derives` or `measures`: narrative coherence or book-level authority $\to$ a formal mechanism or empirical result.

**Audit.** $P$ records the missing proof, model, or measurement certificate. $U$ records any unavailable or version-ambiguous source. $R$ routes the promoted claim back to a paper-level derivation, declared model, or empirical protocol.

| Audit | Valid edge | Invalid edge | Main witness output |
|---|---|---|---|
| FRC 566 | Derivation within $\mathcal O$ | Bookkeeping → universal physical law | $P,R$ |
| FRC 830 | Formal result constrains μ interpretation | Ledger involution proves physical/categorical duality | $P,U$ |
| FRC 787 | Declared latent model implements target dynamics | Diagnostic or model becomes fundamental field | $B,P,R$ |
| Books | Philosophical synthesis interprets and routes | Narrative authority becomes proof or data | $P,U,R$ |

## 8. The books as synthesis nodes

### 8.1 *The Open System (The Unclosed)* v41

This book synthesizes openness, incompleteness, process, and boundary philosophy. Within $G$, its proper strength is integrative: it can relate established external science, FRC questions, and philosophical consequences. Physical statements must still resolve to established sources or status-labeled papers. The book's breadth is not a measurement certificate.

### 8.2 *The Mind in the Coupling* v3.13

This book synthesizes relational cognition and measurable-edge proposals. Its graph record must distinguish functional cognition, candidate mechanisms, reflective witness, reportability, and subjective consciousness. An implemented reporting function can support a functional claim; it does not by itself certify subjective experience.

A book can integrate the corpus story at μ5 and support reflective audit at μ6 without becoming evidence for its own physical propositions. Likewise, the corpus may integrate, monitor, report, and criticize itself without thereby possessing subjective consciousness.

## 9. Valid and prohibited inference

**Valid inference.** If a path from an interpretive node to an empirical node contains no operational measurement, control, uncertainty, and reproducible-analysis certificate, the corpus audit must reject that path as an evidential promotion until the missing record is supplied.

**Prohibited inference.** Because the claim graph finds no unsupported edge, the endpoint is true; because FRC can audit itself, the corpus is self-validating or conscious; or because two books integrate the project, their interpretations prove its physical conjectures.

## 10. Non-results and failure conditions

This paper does not prove a universal μ ontology, an FRC physical law, a fundamental field, a physical ledger lift, Born reciprocity, Majid-style self-duality, corpus completeness, corpus consciousness, or freedom from contradiction. It does not convert author or AI review into independent proof review.

The method must be revised or rejected if:

1. stable claim and version identifiers cannot be maintained;
2. auditors classify the same edge inconsistently without recording the reason;
3. a long dependency path can hide a missing promotion certificate;
4. boundary changes escape $B$ because operational realizations are not recorded;
5. contradictory claims are erased rather than retained in $C$;
6. audit authority replaces examination of the underlying proof, model, or data; or
7. the repair set $R$ cannot name a concrete reopening gate.
