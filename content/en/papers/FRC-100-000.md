## 1. Two registers, one FRC

The FRC program operates in **two explicitly separated registers** that share a discipline of open systems and explicit boundaries while enforcing strict evidential non-transfer:

1. **Physics register — Fractal Resonance Coherence (FRC):** The technical research program built around the canonical reciprocity form $dS + k^*\,d\ln C = 0$, open-system boundary accounting, operational registers (`FRC 700.000–007`), structural obstruction theorems (`FRC 830.000–005`), model-specific computational and physical benchmarks, and the public negative-gate falsification ledger ([https://fractalresonance.com/en/falsifications](https://fractalresonance.com/en/falsifications)). Every claim in this register carries a formal evidence label and explicit failure clauses.
2. **Philosophy register — Fractal Resonance Cognition (FRC):** The philosophical position developed in the 2026 companion monographs *The Open System* (*The Unclosed*) and *The Mind in the Coupling*, which treat mind as sustained recursive coupling and basin dynamics rather than an isolated internal substance.

**Strict non-conflation rule:** Coherence lives in physics; cognition lives in philosophy. The physics relation $dS + k^*\,d\ln C = 0$ does **not** claim to prove consciousness, subjective experience, or a theory of mind, and philosophical synthesis in *The Open System* or *The Mind in the Coupling* supplies **zero** empirical proof for any physical equation or quantum-foundation hypothesis.

## 2. Canonical stance in the physics register

Fractal Resonance Coherence begins from the stance that real physical systems are open and that absolute final stillness is not the default physical picture. Its load-bearing reciprocity form is

$$
dS + k^*\,d\ln C = 0. \tag{1}
$$

The form of Eq. (1) is scale invariant. The starred quantity $k^*$ is the Boltzmann bridge that makes entropy and logarithmic coherence commensurable. It is not an ordinary tunable constant and is never refitted to an outcome, time step, or evolving state. At a declared operational register $\mu$, the same relation may be instantiated as $dS_\mu + k^*_\mu\,d\ln C_\mu = 0$, with channels, units, and boundary fixed before scoring.

FRC uses Eq. (1) operationally as a bookkeeping identity and control-law form at a declared boundary. Its extension as a law of nature governing real open systems generally remains an **open physical conjecture**.

## 3. Boundary and residual discipline

For a modeled subsystem, standard nonequilibrium accounting remains

$$
dS_{\mathrm{sys}} = d_i S + d_e S, \qquad d_i S \ge 0. \tag{2}
$$

Every physical or computational use must declare $S$, $C$, $\mu$, the system boundary, the environment channel, and the sign convention prior to evaluation. An identification such as $\lambda = -d_e S$ is admissible when it follows from that declared accounting convention; it is neither universal without a boundary model nor rejected by fiat.

Movement of the system-only diagnostic

$$
Q_{\mathrm{sys},\mu} = S_{\mathrm{sys},\mu} + k^*_\mu \ln C_\mu \tag{3}
$$

identifies an unclosed system-only ledger. It does not by itself measure irreversible entropy production, quantify bath export, or settle the open-system physical conjecture.

## 4. Lambda notation

Current papers separate three distinct objects previously conflated under a single symbol:

$$
\Lambda_{\mathrm{obs}} = \Lambda_0 \ln C_{\mathrm{obs}}, \qquad \Lambda_{\mathrm{eq}} = \Lambda_0 \ln C_{\mathrm{target}}, \tag{4}
$$

together with an optional latent or coarse-grained dynamical state $\Lambda_{\mathrm{dyn}}$ that relaxes toward $\Lambda_{\mathrm{eq}}$ when finite lag is explicitly modeled and tested against observation-only and equal-feature non-Lambda baselines. A fundamental spacetime Lambda field is a separate conjecture. Evidence for an instantaneous transform $\Lambda_{\mathrm{obs}}$, a calibrated target $\Lambda_{\mathrm{eq}}$, or a useful latent closure $\Lambda_{\mathrm{dyn}}$ is **not** evidence for a fundamental ontology.
