### 3.1 Canonical Law and Operational Instantiation

Following `FRC 566.001 v2.2` (Concept DOI `10.5281/zenodo.17437758`, v2.2 DOI `10.5281/zenodo.21298181`), the canonical scale-invariant statement of entropy-coherence reciprocity is

$$
\boxed{dS + k^*\,d\ln C = 0.} \tag{1}
$$

In Eq. (1), $S$ denotes a declared entropy channel, $C > 0$ denotes a declared positive coherence channel, and $k^* > 0$ is the starred Boltzmann bridge making entropy and $\ln C$ commensurable. Crucially, $k^*$ is **not** a tunable fit parameter, is never fitted to an observed outcome, and is never treated as a dynamically running state variable.

When instantiated in an explicit physical experiment, numerical simulation, or statistical model at a declared operating scale or register $\mu$, Eq. (1) takes the operational form

$$
dS_\mu + k^*_\mu\,d\ln C_\mu = 0, \qquad C_\mu \in (0, 1]. \tag{2}
$$

The subscript $\mu$ records the predeclared unit map, entropy channel, coherence channel, and accounting boundary—not a second law. In natural information units (nats), $k^*_{\mu_{\mathrm{nat}}} = 1$; in standard thermodynamic units, $k^*_{\mu_{\mathrm{th}}} = k_B$. Both channels must be operationally independent: defining $C_\mu := \exp(-S_\mu/k^*_\mu)$ and then claiming Eq. (2) as an empirical discovery is circular and forbidden.

### 3.2 Exact Ledger Conservation and Exponential Trade-Off Scaling

Define the operational ledger potential $Q_\mu(t)$ at a fixed predeclared register $\mu$:

$$
Q_\mu(t) := S_\mu(t) + k^*_\mu \ln C_\mu(t), \qquad dQ_\mu = 0 \;\Longrightarrow\; Q_\mu(t_2) = Q_\mu(t_1). \tag{3}
$$

Integrating Eq. (2) between any two states $t_1$ and $t_2$ along a balanced ledger path ($dQ_\mu = 0$) yields the exact finite-difference trade-off scaling

$$
\frac{C_\mu(t_2)}{C_\mu(t_1)} = \exp\!\left[-\frac{S_\mu(t_2) - S_\mu(t_1)}{k^*_\mu}\right]. \tag{4}
$$

Equation (4) is an exact algebraic consequence of Eq. (2) and Eq. (3). It shows that any lawful positive rescaling of entropy units $(S_\mu, k^*_\mu) \mapsto (\alpha S_\mu, \alpha k^*_\mu)$ with $\alpha > 0$ leaves the dimensionless exponent and coherence ratio invariant. Introducing normalized ledger coordinates

$$

eliminates all unit-representation dependence: every lawful unit map collapses onto the single dimensionless operational cone $M_+$ in Eq. (5).
