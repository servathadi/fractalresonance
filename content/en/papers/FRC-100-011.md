- **`[same-shape-as]`** — mathematically the same object, borrowed wholesale; FRC claims no novelty.

## 4. The reciprocity relation

$$
dS + k^*\,d\ln C = 0. \tag{1}
$$

When the operational instantiation holds at that boundary, $\lambda_{B,\mu} = -d_e S_\mu$; under another boundary or residual convention, that identification need not follow. Every use must predeclare $S$, $C$, $\mu$, boundary, environment channel, and sign convention.

## 10. False friends — terms a physicist will misread

FRC reuses common words with narrowed operational meanings. Do **not** import everyday or unrelated technical meanings:

- **Coherence vs Cognition:** In the physics register, $C$ ("coherence") is an operational order parameter in $(0,1]$—**not** the speed of light $c$, **not** heat capacity, and **not** "consciousness." Cognition belongs to the philosophy register (*The Open System*, *The Mind in the Coupling*).
- **$\Lambda_{\mathrm{obs}}$, $\Lambda_{\mathrm{eq}}$, and $\Lambda_{\mathrm{dyn}}$:** Three distinct objects. Never assign independent dynamics to the instantaneous transform $\Lambda_{\mathrm{obs}}$ or equate a useful latent closure with a fundamental field.
- **$k^*$ and $k^*_\mu$:** $k^*$ is the scale-invariant starred Boltzmann bridge; $k^*_\mu$ indexes a declared operational realization and units. Neither is an outcome-fitted parameter or evolving state variable.
- **Operational registers ($\mu_0$–$\mu_7$ in `700.000–007`):** Boundary-indexed scope conventions governed by a one-way evidence rule—**not** a physical scale parameter, **not** a causal hierarchy, and **not** a cross-register proof shortcut.
- **Structural obstruction (`830.005 v0.3`):** An exact present-structure theorem under explicit hypotheses (such as nonempty open sets on a qubit Bloch ball)—**not** a rhetorical dismissal and **not** a universal impossibility proof for explicitly open empty-interior, projective, or $d \ge 3$ gates.
- **"Collapse" and "resonance":** Collapse denotes a candidate finite-time resonant relaxation into pointer attractors (not gravitational or observer-caused Copenhagen collapse); resonance denotes frequency-matched mode coupling (not mystical "vibration").
- **"Maximize coherence":** Not the physical regime of interest; live open-system dynamics sit in the intermediate non-closing band, never at the frozen $C \to 1$ limit.
