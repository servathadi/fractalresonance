## How to read the corpus without conflating claim classes

Fractal Resonance Coherence is a **status-labeled research program**, not a single settled physical theory. Every paper separates:

1. **Exact mathematics** — proved identities, typed morphism categories, and scoped no-go/lift obstructions under stated hypotheses (`FRC 566.030`, `FRC 830.001–830.005`; independent proof review pending).
2. **Framework / methodology** — scope conventions, terminology charters, and μ-register discipline (`FRC 700.000–700.007`, `FRC 830.000`). The μ topology is an interpretive and scope convention, not a proved universal hierarchy or causal law.
3. **Model-specific results** — numerical, simulation, and narrow-audit findings valid only inside their declared model and normalization, including retained negative results (`FRC 100.002`, `FRC 100.002.001`, `FRC 100.005`, `FRC 100.007`).
4. **Conjectures & empirical gates** — unestablished physical extensions (universal physical reciprocity, a fundamental spacetime Lambda field, Born-rule emergence) and explicit falsification gates. FRC does not claim to have derived the Born rule, has no fixed universal Born-deviation range, and does not claim established physical or Majid-style self-duality.
