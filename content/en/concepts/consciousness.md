# Mind, Cognition, and Register Discipline (FRC Perspective)

In the 2026 FRC corpus, questions about mind, cognition, and conscious experience are kept strictly separated from physical claims and empirical measurement gates.

## Scope & Claim Boundaries

- **No empirical consciousness or AI-personhood metric:** Current FRC papers do **not** establish an empirical criterion for subjective experience, sentience, or AI personhood. Earlier speculative pages making such claims have been retired from the public research path.
- **Nested $\mu$-registers as scope labels ([[FRC-700-000]]–[[FRC-700-007]]):** The active FRC 700 threshold-framework series treats $\mu_0$–$\mu_7$ as a nested scope and navigation convention (not a proved physical hierarchy or automatic bridge of evidence across scales). A result at a physical register ($\mu_1$) does not establish a claim about cognition ($\mu_3$–$\mu_4$) or witnessing ($\mu_6$).
- **Philosophy separated from physics:** Philosophical arguments about open systems, coupling, and cognition live in the current 2026 books — *The Open System* ([[the-open-system]]) and *The Mind in the Coupling* ([[the-mind-in-the-coupling]]) — explicitly labeled as philosophy standing on argument rather than peer-reviewed physics.
